We are happy to announce that we have gym permits and are anticipating a normal season for the 2022/2023 season.  The season will get started on September 21st and will run until the end of April, extending our normal season by two weeks. 

 

Fees will remain the same as they were in 2019 at $650.  The schools are also the same, J.W. Gerth, Courtland Avenue, Baden, Breslau, Lester B. Pearson, and Northlake Woods.  The format of play will remain the same, co-ed (3 girls, 3 men), 2 matches per night (6 games), Wednesday nights starting at 7:15PM.  Our league is typically divided into 4 divisions with 9 teams in each division, this gives everyone a chance to play teams at their own level. 

 

We are full, however if you would like to be added to the waitlist please reach out to Dave O’Neil (valleyboy77_26@hotmail.com) and/or Tom Shaw (tshaw@tigercat.com) if you are interested in joining the league
